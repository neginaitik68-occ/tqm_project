TQM INTERNAL CUSTOMER

Owner / Manager as an Internal Customer

The restaurant owner or manager is an internal customer because the billing system provides information and services required for effective restaurant management.

The system should help management monitor billing operations, reduce errors, improve efficiency and support better service quality.

01

Owner / Manager Requirements

Quality requirements identified from the management perspective.

📊

Easy Monitoring

Management should be able to understand billing operations easily.

💰

Accurate Billing

Bills and financial calculations should be accurate and consistent.

❌

Reduced Errors

The system should reduce mistakes during billing and checkout.

⚡

Efficient Operations

Billing activities should be completed efficiently.

♿

Accessible System

The system should remain usable for people with different accessibility needs.

😊

Customer Satisfaction

Better billing quality should contribute to a better customer experience.

02

Management Quality Objectives

Objectives that support continuous quality improvement.

Requirement Quality Objective Measurement
Easy Monitoring Provide clear system information. Ease of use
Accurate Billing Maintain correct calculations. Calculation accuracy
Reduced Errors Prevent invalid billing operations. Number of billing errors
Efficient Operations Reduce unnecessary billing steps. Process efficiency
Accessibility Make the interface usable by more users. Accessibility features
Customer Satisfaction Improve the overall service experience. Customer feedback
