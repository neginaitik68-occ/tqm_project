EXTERNAL CUSTOMER

Restaurant Customer

The restaurant customer is the external customer of the billing system. The system should provide an accurate, simple, clear and accessible billing experience.

Customer Requirements

🧾

Accurate Bill

Customers expect all item prices, quantities, tax and final totals to be calculated correctly.

Quality Objective: Reduce billing errors
🖥️

Simple Billing

Customers should be able to understand the billing process without unnecessary complexity.

Quality Objective: Simplify the interface
👀

Clear Bill

Selected items, quantities, prices, tax and total should be clearly visible.

Quality Objective: Improve information clarity
⚡

Easy Checkout

Customers should be able to complete the billing process efficiently.

Quality Objective: Reduce unnecessary steps
♿

Accessibility

The system is being developed to support different accessibility needs.

Planned: Days 24–27
