EXTERNAL CUSTOMER

Restaurant Customer

The restaurant customer is the primary external customer of the Restaurant Billing System.

The purpose of this customer module is to connect customer requirements with the quality goals of the billing system.

Customer Requirements

The system is designed according to the needs and expectations of the restaurant customer.

🧾

Accurate Billing

Customers expect the bill to contain correct item prices, quantities, subtotal, tax and final total.

Quality: Accuracy
👁️

Easy-to-Read Bill

Billing information should be clearly displayed so that customers can understand their charges easily.

Quality: Readability
⚡

Fast Service

The billing process should be quick and efficient to reduce unnecessary waiting time.

Quality: Efficiency
♿

Accessible Interface

The system should be usable by customers with different accessibility requirements.

Quality: Accessibility
🔍

Clear Information

Menu items, prices, quantities, tax and final amounts should be presented clearly.

Quality: Clarity
😊

Customer Satisfaction

The overall billing experience should meet customer expectations and provide a satisfactory service.

Quality: Satisfaction

Quality Commitments

Customer requirements are converted into quality commitments using the TQM customer-focus principle.

01

Accuracy

Automatic calculations and bill validation help reduce calculation errors.

02

Accessibility

Accessibility features will be added to make the system easier to use.

03

Simplicity

The billing process should contain simple and understandable steps.

04

Reliability

Invalid billing information should be detected before checkout.

05

Efficiency

The system should reduce unnecessary manual work during billing.

06

Continuous Improvement

Testing and customer feedback will be used to improve system quality.
