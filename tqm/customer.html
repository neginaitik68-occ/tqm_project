Restaurant Customer Module

The restaurant customer is an external customer whose requirements are used to define the quality of the billing system.

Customer Requirements

Accurate Bill

The customer expects all item prices, quantities, tax and total amounts to be calculated correctly.

Simple Billing

The billing process should be easy to understand and use.

Clear Bill

The customer should be able to clearly understand the items and final amount.

Easy Checkout

The checkout process should be simple and straightforward.

Accessibility

The system should be accessible to users with different visual and interaction needs.

Customer Quality Expectations

Requirement System Feature Expected Quality Result
Accurate Bill Automatic calculations Correct bill amount
Simple Billing Easy item selection Faster billing
Clear Bill Bill summary Easy understanding
Easy Checkout Checkout function Simple transaction completion
Accessibility Accessibility features Better usability
