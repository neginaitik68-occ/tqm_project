TQM Customers & Stakeholders

Total Quality Management focuses on understanding the needs of all customers and stakeholders who interact with the restaurant billing system.

Restaurant Customer

External Customer

The restaurant customer is the person who purchases food or services and receives the final bill.

Main Expectations

  • Accurate billing
  • Clear bill information
  • Simple checkout
  • Fast service
  • Accessible interface
Open Customer Module →

Cashier / Staff

Internal Customer

Cashiers and restaurant staff use the billing system to create bills and process customer transactions.

Main Expectations

  • Fast item selection
  • Easy quantity handling
  • Reliable calculations
  • Simple bill management
  • Easy navigation
Open Cashier Module →

Owner / Manager

Internal Customer

The restaurant owner or manager is responsible for monitoring business operations and overall system quality.

Main Expectations

  • Reliable billing
  • Quality monitoring
  • Customer satisfaction
  • Error reduction
  • Continuous improvement
