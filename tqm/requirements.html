Customer Requirements & Quality Objectives

TQM begins by identifying customer requirements and converting them into measurable quality objectives for the billing system.

Identified Customer Requirements

1. Accurate Billing

The system must calculate item prices, quantities, tax and total amounts correctly.

2. Fast Billing

The cashier should be able to add and manage items quickly.

3. Simple Interface

The system should be easy to understand and operate.

4. Easy Checkout

Completing a transaction should require minimal effort.

5. Accessibility

The interface should support users with different accessibility requirements.

6. Reliability

The billing system should operate consistently and reduce errors.
