Customer Requirements

TQM begins by understanding customer needs and converting those needs into measurable quality objectives.

The Restaurant Billing System identifies requirements for external customers, internal staff and restaurant management.

Restaurant Customer Requirements

Accurate Bill

Customers expect the bill to contain correct prices, quantities, tax and final total.

Quality Objective:

Minimize billing calculation errors.

Simple Billing

Customers should be able to understand the billing process easily.

Quality Objective:

Provide a simple and understandable billing interface.

Clear Bill

Selected items, quantities and charges should be clearly displayed.

Quality Objective:

Improve clarity of billing information.

Easy Checkout

The billing process should allow quick and simple checkout.

Quality Objective:

Reduce unnecessary steps during checkout.

Cashier / Staff Requirements

Fast Billing

Cashiers need to create bills quickly during restaurant operations.

Quality Objective:

Reduce the number of steps required for billing.

Easy Item Selection

Menu items should be easy to identify and add to the bill.

Quality Objective:

Simplify order entry.

Quantity Control

Cashiers should easily increase or decrease item quantities.

Quality Objective:

Make bill corrections simple.

Reduced Errors

The system should identify invalid billing conditions.

Quality Objective:

Improve billing reliability.

Owner / Manager Requirements

Accurate Billing Information

Management requires reliable billing calculations.

Quality Objective:

Maintain consistent billing accuracy.

Efficient Operations

The system should simplify restaurant billing operations.

Quality Objective:

Improve operational efficiency.

Quality Monitoring

Management should be able to identify quality problems and improvement areas.

Quality Objective:

Support continuous quality improvement.

Accessibility

The system should become usable by customers with different accessibility needs.

Quality Objective:

Improve system accessibility.
