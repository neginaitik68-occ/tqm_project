The requirements of external and internal customers are converted into measurable quality objectives.

Key Quality Objectives

01

Accuracy

Subtotal, tax and total should be calculated correctly.

02

Accessibility

The system should be usable by customers with different accessibility needs.

03

Efficiency

The billing process should require minimal unnecessary steps.

04

Reliability

Invalid billing transactions should be prevented.

05

Customer Satisfaction

The system should satisfy external and internal customer requirements.

06

Continuous Improvement

System quality should be regularly evaluated and improved.
