INTERNAL CUSTOMER

Cashier / Staff

The cashier or staff member is an internal customer of the Restaurant Billing System. The system should help staff create and manage bills quickly and accurately.

Cashier / Staff Requirements

⚡

Fast Billing

Cashiers should be able to create bills quickly during restaurant operations.

Quality Objective: Reduce billing steps
🍔

Easy Item Selection

Menu items should be clearly displayed and easy to add to the bill.

Quality Objective: Simplify order entry
🔢

Quantity Control

Staff should be able to increase or decrease quantities easily.

Quality Objective: Simplify bill corrections
🗑️

Easy Bill Management

Incorrect items should be easy to remove or the complete bill should be easy to clear.

Quality Objective: Reduce correction effort
🛡️

Reduced Errors

The system should identify invalid billing conditions before checkout.

Quality Objective: Improve billing reliability
