INTERNAL CUSTOMER

Cashier / Staff

The cashier or staff member is an internal customer who uses the Restaurant Billing System to create, manage and process customer bills.

TQM focuses on understanding the needs of internal customers because efficient internal processes contribute to better service for external customers.

Cashier Requirements

The billing system is designed to support the following needs of the cashier.

⚡

Fast Billing Process

The cashier should be able to add menu items and prepare a bill quickly without unnecessary steps.

Quality: Efficiency
🖱️

Easy Item Selection

Menu items should be easy to identify and add to the bill.

Quality: Usability
🔢

Automatic Calculations

Subtotal, tax and final total should be calculated automatically.

Quality: Accuracy
🛡️

Reduced Errors

The system should detect invalid quantities and billing information before checkout.

Quality: Reliability
🧾

Easy Bill Management

The cashier should be able to increase, decrease, remove and clear bill items easily.

Quality: Simplicity
♿

Accessible Interface

The interface should support accessible operation for staff members with different needs.

Quality: Accessibility

Current System Support

The existing billing system already provides several functions that support cashier requirements.

🍔

Menu Selection

Menu items are displayed dynamically for quick selection.

➕

Add Items

Cashiers can add selected menu items directly to the bill.

🔢

Quantity Control

Item quantities can be increased or decreased during billing.

🧮

Automatic Total

Subtotal, tax and total are calculated automatically.

❌

Remove Items

Individual items can be removed from the bill when required.

✅

Bill Validation

The system validates the bill before checkout to reduce invalid transactions.
